# v1.7.2 contention outcome

The separately preregistered `v1.7.2` contention diagnostic completed all 15
trials without an invalid run and passed every frozen safety and efficiency
gate.

| Measure | `v1.7.2` result | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |

| Accepted overlap scenarios | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| External mutations | 10 | Exactly one per trial |

| Low-risk adapter calls | 0 | 0 |

| Median paired input-token change vs `v1.6.0` | -48.75% | At most -20% |

Checkpoint adherence increased from 6/10 in `v1.7.1` to 10/10 in `v1.7.2`.
The correction replaced an optional-looking multi-command claim sequence with
one `checkpoint-prepare` command. It creates the opaque blueprint, submits all
holder claims concurrently, and fails closed unless exactly one winner and
all losing dispositions are confirmed before the external write.

This remains an exploratory second-remediation diagnostic. The destination
and overlap are synthetic, the conditions ran at different times against the
single production app, and one model/reasoning setting does not establish
universal performance. It is not pooled with the main 200-run publication.
